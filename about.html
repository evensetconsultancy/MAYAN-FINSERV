About us

Mayan Finserv Solutions

A loan distribution partner in Adoni that puts the borrower first.

Mayan Finserv Solutions was founded by Viswanadha Achari to give families and small businesses in Adoni and the surrounding districts what customers in big cities take for granted: a knowledgeable, independent advisor who compares lenders and stands with the borrower through the whole process.

What we do

We are a channel partner and referral agent for nationalised banks, private banks, housing finance companies and NBFCs. We assess your profile, pick the lender whose policy fits it, prepare the file the way that lender's credit team expects, follow it through sanction and disbursal, and stay available for everything after: statements, part payments, NOC, closure.

What we do not do

  • We do not lend money ourselves or take deposits.
  • We do not charge the customer a fee for consultation or file processing. Our income is the payout from the lender on disbursal.
  • We do not send your file to multiple lenders at once. One well placed application protects your credit score.
  • We do not promise approvals. We tell you the realistic chance and what would improve it.

Where we work

Our office is on Aspari Road in Adoni. We serve customers across Kurnool, Nandyal, Bellary and Raichur districts with doorstep document collection, and handle files from anywhere in Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and Karnataka over WhatsApp and email.

Our commitments

  • Written disclosure of the lender's processing fee and all charges before you accept
  • Reply to every enquiry within one working hour
  • Your documents are shared only with the lender you approve
  • Plain language explanation of every clause in the sanction letter
