Frequently asked

Will a credit card hurt my CIBIL score?

Not if you pay the full statement amount on time and use less than 30 to 40 percent of the limit. Used this way it improves your score.

What is the interest if I pay the minimum due?

Typically 36 to 42 percent per year. Never pay only the minimum for long; convert large spends to EMI or take a personal loan instead.

Which card should I take?

Tell us your top three monthly spends. Fuel, groceries and online shopping each have a card that pays back the most on that category.
